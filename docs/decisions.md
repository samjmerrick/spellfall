| 2026-11-20 | Shift in the UI | Shift is a drag: pick up one of your own units that the engine offers a Shift for (`legalActions`), drop it on an empty slot in another lane. The planned move shows as an outlined copy in the destination, a faded unit with an arrow at the source, and a chip above the hand (tap either the outline or the chip to take it back). | 2026-11-20 | Lane balance: what is measured | `pnpm sim:balance` has a Lanes section: per lane effect, how often it appears, the share of units and mana played into it against the other two lanes of the same matches (rounds 3 to 8, when every lane is revealed, so a late-revealed lane is not penalised), hero damage dealt through its combat per match (x the mean over all lanes; End of Round hero damage is not counted), the win rate of the player who dealt more hero damage there, and the draw rate while it is present. A lane with fewer than 100 appearances is not judged. Out-of-target lanes need a reason in `balance-exceptions.json` (`lanes`), like cards. |
| 2026-11-20 | Lane balance: target band | Unit share 20-55% (an even split is 33%), hero damage 0.4-1.8x the mean, damage leader wins at most 80% (a lane that decides the match), draws at most 8%. The spec's example band of 25-42% was widened: Normal is damage-greedy (hero HP is worth 3 points, a board point 0.5), so any lane that raises hero damage draws well over a third of the units however it is tuned, and a 25-42% band would need exceptions for a third of the pool. The band still catches lanes that are avoided (before: Glacier 9%, Lowlands 9%, Sanctuary 6%) or that swallow the match (Gorge, Crucible, Chasm at 70-80%). |
| 2026-11-20 | Lane balance: do the bots see lane effects? | Yes. Normal scores each candidate plan by simulating the round with the real engine, so overflow multipliers, hero immunity, End of Round effects, caps and every new hook are in the score; Hard does the same against sampled replies. Two experiments changed nothing, so the AI and `ai-weights.json` are unchanged: (1) scoring each finalist against an empty reply and against a mirror of its own plan, averaged, moved no lane's unit share by more than 4 points; (2) leaving lane stat bonuses out of the static board score made health lanes less popular and power lanes no less. The remaining blind spot is Normal's assumption that the opponent plays nothing new; Hard samples replies. The data therefore reflects what the lanes do, not AI blindness. |
